PhotoAt: GPS Camera Timestamp

Effective Date: 26 November 2026
Sections: 1. Overview 2. Data Collection 3. Permissions 4. Third Parties 5. Data Deletion 6. Contact

Zero Telemetry & Absolute Device Privacy

PhotoAt: GPS Camera Timestamp operates entirely on your physical hardware. MHPartners does not collect, sell, harvest, or transmit your personal data, keystrokes, app preferences, or telemetry to external servers. Your device is your sanctuary.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to PhotoAt: GPS Camera Timestamp (package: net.vertexapps.gpstimestamp), engineered by Vertex Apps and published by MHPartners (“Studio”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

PhotoAt stamps photos and videos with GPS coordinates, street addresses, mini-maps, verified timestamps, and field notes. All captures, projects, notes, and verification hashes remain strictly on your device—with zero user accounts.

2. Information Collection and On-Device Storage

  • No User Accounts or Cloud Uploads: PhotoAt requires no registration, sign-in, email, or phone number. Your photos, videos, field notes, project folders, Saved Sites, SHA-256 verification hashes, and exported reports (PDF, ZIP, KMZ, GeoJSON, CSV) are created and stored exclusively on your phone (Room SQLite & DataStore; cloud auto-backup is disabled).
  • Ephemeral Location for Maps, Addresses & Weather: When you have an active internet connection and request a street address, mini-map tile, or weather stamp, the app sends your device’s current coordinates ephemerally via encrypted HTTPS to retrieve that information. Coordinates are not stored by us on any remote server.
  • Zero First-Party Analytics: Vertex Apps does not operate proprietary behavioral analytics or user-profiling servers.

3. Android System Permissions

Camera & Microphone (CAMERA / RECORD_AUDIO / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT)

Used strictly to capture stamped photos and 1080p videos (including optional Bluetooth headset microphone routing) on your device.

Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Used to stamp GPS coordinates, altitude, speed, Saved Sites geofences, and fetch street addresses, map tiles, and local weather. Mock-location apps are blocked in Verified Mode.

Gallery Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO)

Used only when you pick existing gallery photos/videos to Retro-Stamp or save stamped media and field reports to your device.

Network & Haptics (INTERNET / VIBRATE)

Internet is used for map tiles, address lookup, weather, SNTP time sync, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob ads. VIBRATE provides optional shutter feedback.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Map, Address, Weather & Time Services:

  • OpenStreetMap & Reverse Geocoding: Requests map tiles and street addresses for your stamp coordinates (© OpenStreetMap contributors).
  • Open-Meteo Weather: Sends coordinates ephemerally to fetch temperature and weather conditions for weather stamps.
  • SNTP Network Time Sync: Queries public network time servers so Verified Mode timestamps cannot be spoofed via phone clock changes.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes optional Lifetime Pro purchases securely; MHPartners never sees or stores payment details.

Advertising (Google AdMob): PhotoAt displays advertisements (such as banner, interstitial, and/or optional rewarded ads) powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, device model, OS version, app usage related to ads, and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and—where consented—personalized ads. Review How Google uses partner data and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent prompt via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are served. We never sell personal data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your projects, Saved Sites, verification records, and settings remain exclusively on your device and are permanently deleted when you clear app storage (Android Settings → Apps → PhotoAt → Storage) or uninstall the app. Photos and videos saved to your public gallery can be deleted in your Photos app.

6. Children’s Privacy

PhotoAt: GPS Camera Timestamp is a general-audience field-documentation and photography utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
