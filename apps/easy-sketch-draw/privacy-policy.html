01 Overview and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Easy Sketch Draw handles information. It applies to the Android application Easy Sketch Draw, published by MHPartners (“Studio”, “we”, “our”, “us”), and to this page.

Easy Sketch Draw is a trace-and-colour drawing game for children aged roughly 4–10 and for casual artists. It is built for a family audience and is designed to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the children’s provisions of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

02 The short version

  • We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, birth date or any account.
  • There is no login, no cloud profile and no social feature.
  • Progress, coins, stars and saved drawings stay on your device.
  • We do not use the Android Advertising ID — the permission is removed from the app.
  • Advertisements are family-safe and non-personalised. We do not build profiles of children.
  • Everything that leads outside the game — links, purchases, sharing, saving to the gallery — sits behind a grown-ups only gate.
  • You can delete everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

03 Information we do not collect

We do not collect, request or store personal information from children or adults. In particular:

  • No account data. No registration, login, username, email address, phone number or social profile.
  • No sensitive device access. The app never accesses the camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS or precise location.
  • No advertising identifier. The com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission is removed from the application manifest, in line with Google Play Families requirements.
  • No behavioural profiling. We do not build advertising or interest profiles, and we do not sell personal data.

04 Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, on your device only, using Android’s standard application storage:

  • completed levels, earned stars and coins;
  • unlocked marker skins and daily reward streaks;
  • drawings saved in My Artworks;
  • preferences such as sound, vibration and language.

This information never leaves the device and is not uploaded to us or to any cloud service. It is removed when you clear the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Easy Sketch Draw → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app.

06 Third-party services

The app uses a small number of services. Each is configured for a child audience.

Google AdMob — family-safe, non-personalised advertising

Advertisements are marked as child-directed and are non-personalised. Banners never cover the drawing canvas while a child is playing. Behavioural profiling and the Advertising ID are disabled. AdMob may still process limited technical information — such as IP address, device model, operating system version and ad interaction events — to deliver contextual ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and prevent fraud, under Google’s own policies.

Optional rewarded videos

A player may choose to watch a family-safe video to unlock a temporary marker skin for one level, to receive an extra hint, or to skip a level. Rewarded ads are always optional and never required to play.

Google Play Billing — purchases

Optional one-time purchases (such as VIP / Remove Ads, marker bundles and coin packs) are processed by Google Play and sit behind the grown-ups only gate. We never receive or store card numbers or payment account details.

Microsoft Clarity — usage analytics and diagnostics

Clarity helps us understand how the game is used and to find crashes, layout problems and points of confusion. It may collect aggregated, non-identifying interaction data such as screen transitions, tap heatmaps, session length, device model, operating system version, app version and technical network metadata. This is used only to improve the app — never for advertising or profiling — and we do not sell or share it for marketing. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Microsoft Clarity Terms of Use.

Level content server

The app may download new drawing level definitions. No identifiers or tracking tokens are sent with those requests.

If a service is added or removed in a future version, this section will be updated before that version is published.

07 Children’s privacy and Google Play Families

Easy Sketch Draw is directed to children and families. We apply the following commitments:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and none is required to play.
  • We do not serve personalised or interest-based advertising, and we do not use the Advertising ID.
  • Ad content is family-safe and appropriate for children.
  • There are no social features, no chat, no user-generated content sharing and no public profiles.
  • External links, support emails, artwork sharing or saving, and purchases are all placed behind a parental gate that a young child cannot pass.
  • We do not condition play on the disclosure of more information than is reasonably necessary.
  • We comply with COPPA, with GDPR Article 8 for children in the EU, and with the Google Play Families Policy.

08 Choices and controls for parents

  • Parental gate. A “grown-ups only” arithmetic challenge protects settings that lead outside the game: links, purchases, sharing and saving to the gallery.
  • Sound and haptics. Vibration and sound effects can be changed in Settings at any time.
  • Delete local data. Clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. All progress and saved artwork is removed permanently.
  • Remove individual drawings. Saved drawings can be deleted inside My Artworks.
  • Ask us a question or make a request. A parent or guardian may write to support@mhpartners.com to ask what information we hold about their child, or to ask us to delete it. Because the app has no accounts and stores nothing on our servers, in most cases we will hold no information about a child at all — and we will confirm that in writing.

09 Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store payment details. Refunds and billing questions are handled by Google Play support, in line with Google’s policies. Purchases are reachable only through the parental gate.

10 Data retention

We keep no personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to retain. Information created inside the game stays on the device until it is deleted by clearing app storage or uninstalling.

Third-party services keep technical data for their own limited periods, under their own policies (Google and Microsoft, linked above).

11 International transfers

Because the app has no accounts and no server-side storage of personal data, we do not transfer personal data between countries. Where a third-party service such as AdMob or Clarity processes technical data, it does so under its own safeguards and standard contractual clauses.

12 Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes — for example, if a new service is added or a permission is introduced — this policy will be updated before that version is released. The effective date at the top will change, and material changes will be noted in the release notes on Google Play.
