Privacy Policy

Easy Sketch Draw

Effective Date: October 2026
Sections: 1. Overview 2. Data Collection 3. Permissions 4. Third Parties 5. Data Deletion 6. Contact

Zero Telemetry & Absolute Device Privacy

Easy Sketch Draw operates entirely on your physical hardware. MHPartners does not collect, sell, harvest, or transmit your personal data, keystrokes, app preferences, or telemetry to external servers. Your device is your sanctuary.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Easy Sketch Draw, published by MHPartners (“Studio”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

Easy Sketch Draw is a child-friendly trace-and-color drawing game designed for children (ages 4–10) and casual artists. It is built in strict compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy. For privacy questions, parents or guardians can reach us at support@mhpartners.com or privacy@vertexapps.net.

2. Information Collection and Local Storage

Easy Sketch Draw does not collect, request, or store personally identifiable information (PII) from children or adults.

  • No User Accounts or Cloud Profiles: There is no registration, login, username, email address, phone number, or social profile required to play.
  • No Sensitive Sensor Access: The app never accesses your device camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, or GPS location.
  • No Advertising ID (AD_ID Removed): Consistent with Google Play Families requirements, the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission is explicitly removed from the application manifest.
  • On-Device Local Progress & Artwork: Completed levels, earned stars, coins, unlocked marker skins, daily reward streaks, saved drawings (My Artworks), and preferences (sound effects, vibration, language) are saved strictly on your local device via shared_preferences. None of this data is uploaded to any cloud server.

3. Android System Permissions

Easy Sketch Draw requests only minimal permissions necessary for drawing, feedback, and optional artwork saving:

Haptic Feedback (VIBRATE)

Provides short, gentle vibrations when tracing lines, filling regions, and completing drawings. Can be disabled anytime in Settings.

Network Access (INTERNET)

Used to download new drawing levels from our level catalog and load family-safe, non-personalized advertisements. Bundled levels remain playable offline.

Save Artwork (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 10-)

Used only on Android 10 and older when you explicitly tap Save to export a finished drawing to your Photos gallery, protected behind the Parental Gate.

Parental Gate Protection

A “Grown-ups only” math challenge blocks children from accessing external links, support emails, artwork sharing/saving, or in-app purchases.

4. Third-Party Services, Family-Safe Ads & Purchases

Family-Safe Non-Personalized Advertising (Google AdMob): Easy Sketch Draw uses Google AdMob configured strictly for child-directed treatment and non-personalized, G-rated advertisements only. Banners never appear on the drawing canvas while playing. While behavioral profiling and the Android Advertising ID are disabled, Google AdMob may process limited technical device information (such as IP address, device model, OS version, and ad interaction events) solely to serve contextual ads, cap ad frequency, and prevent fraud under Google’s Partner Sites Policy.

Optional Rewarded Videos: Players may optionally choose to watch a family-safe rewarded video ad to unlock a temporary marker skin for one level, receive an extra hint after the 3 free hints, or skip a level. Rewarded ads are always optional.

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): Optional one-time upgrades (such as VIP / Remove Ads, marker bundles, and coin packs) are processed securely through Google Play Billing and are strictly gated behind a Grown-Ups Only Parental Gate. MHPartners never receives or stores credit card or payment account details.

Level Content Server: The app may fetch newly published drawing level definitions from our level server. No personal identifiers or user tracking tokens are transmitted during level downloads.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All drawings, stars, coins, and preferences stay on your local device:

  • In-App Gallery Management: Players can remove saved drawings from My Artworks at any time inside the app.
  • System Clear Data / Uninstall: Clearing app storage in Android Settings → Apps → Easy Sketch Draw → Storage or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local progress and saved artwork.

6. Children’s Privacy & Google Play Families Compliance

Easy Sketch Draw is designed for children and families and adheres to COPPA, GDPR-K, and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, do not serve personalized ads, block the Advertising ID permission, and place all external links, purchases, and social share actions behind a Grown-Ups Only Parental Gate. Parents with questions may contact us at support@mhpartners.com.
