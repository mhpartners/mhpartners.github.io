Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools

Zero Telemetry & Absolute Device Privacy

Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools operates entirely on your physical hardware. MHPartners does not collect, sell, harvest, or transmit your personal data, keystrokes, app preferences, or telemetry to external servers. Your device is your sanctuary.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools (package: net.vertexapps.wifianalyzer), engineered by Vertex Apps and MHPartners (“Studio”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools is built on a private-by-design architecture: no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no battery-draining background scanning. For any privacy questions, please contact privacy@vertexapps.net or support@mhpartners.com.

2. Information Collection and On-Device Storage

Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools collects zero personally identifiable information (PII). All Wi-Fi and local network data stays exclusively on your phone:

  • Network Names, Router Addresses & Location Never Leave Your Phone: SSIDs, BSSIDs, nearby access points, router/gateway addresses, and device location are processed strictly locally on your device and are never transmitted to Studio servers.
  • No Accounts, Analytics, or Tracking: There is no account creation, login, email collection, analytics SDK, or behavioral tracking.
  • Local History, Saved Devices & CSV Export: Per-network signal and speed history, saved Wake-on-LAN devices, and theme/audio preferences are saved locally on your phone. CSV exports (filterable by network and date range) are saved strictly to the local storage path you choose.
  • Battery-Friendly Widget Updates: Home-screen widgets update live while the app is open, when Wi-Fi connects, on tap, and every 30 minutes—without continuous background scanning.

3. Android System Permissions

To perform local Wi-Fi analysis and user-initiated network diagnostics on Android, the app requests only minimal permissions:

Wi-Fi Scanning & Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES)

Required by Android solely to scan nearby Wi-Fi networks and read current SSID, channel, and band details. Your physical location is never logged or sent off your phone.

Wi-Fi & Network State (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / CHANGE_WIFI_STATE)

Used locally to inspect your live signal gauge, link speed, security type, router path, and provide one-tap network switching.

Internet & LAN Access (INTERNET)

Used for local LAN tools (device scan, port check, Wake-on-LAN) and user-initiated tools (Ping, Traceroute, DNS, Whois), plus consent-gated M-Lab Speed Test and Public IP lookup.

Signal Meter Vibration (VIBRATE)

Optional haptic feedback while using the walk-around Signal Meter finder.

4. Third-Party Services & Consent-Gated Tools

Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools includes zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising SDKs, and zero background tracking. Only two features contact third-party servers, and both ask for your explicit consent first:

  • Speed Test (Measurement Lab / M-Lab): When you consent and run a speed test, the app connects to Measurement Lab (M-Lab) servers to measure download, upload, latency, and jitter. M-Lab processes your IP address and test measurements in accordance with M-Lab’s Privacy Policy.
  • Public IP & Provider Lookup: When you consent to run a Public IP lookup, the app queries an external IP lookup endpoint to display your public IP address and ISP/provider information.

User-Initiated Network Queries: When you manually execute Ping, Traceroute, DNS lookup/comparison (against Cloudflare, Google, and Quad9), or Whois queries, the domain name or IP address you enter is sent directly to the respective resolver or Whois server solely to display the diagnostic result. Home-network tools (connected devices scan, port check, Wake-on-LAN, subnet calculator) operate locally on your LAN.

5. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

Since all history and saved devices are stored locally on your hardware, you remain in full control:

  • In-App Controls: Clear saved history, Wake-on-LAN devices, or consent choices inside the app at any time.
  • System Clear Data / Uninstall: Navigating to Android Settings → Apps → Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools → Storage → Clear Data or uninstalling removes all local databases and preferences permanently.

6. Children’s Privacy & Family Safety

Wifi Analyzer and Network Tools is a general-audience network diagnostic utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in compliance with COPPA.
