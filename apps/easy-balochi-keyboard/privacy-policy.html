Privacy Policy

Balochi Keyboard

Effective Date: October 2026
Sections: 1. Overview 2. Data Collection 3. Permissions 4. Third Parties 5. Data Deletion 6. Contact

Zero Telemetry & Absolute Device Privacy

Balochi Keyboard operates entirely on your physical hardware. MHPartners does not collect, sell, harvest, or transmit your personal data, keystrokes, app preferences, or telemetry to external servers. Your device is your sanctuary.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the official Android mobile application Balochi Keyboard engineered by MHPartners ("Studio", "we", "our", or "us").

Balochi Keyboard is a smart dual-language keyboard for Android with intelligent suggestions, full Balochi special characters, voice typing, custom themes, and complete privacy. Our studio architecture follows the strict principle of minimal privilege and complete local isolation. We believe that modern mobile applications do not require cloud surveillance to provide exceptional user utility.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Balochi Keyboard does not collect, store, or log personally identifiable information (PII). All user-generated configurations, workflows, and inputs remain exclusively within your device's private sandbox:

  • Local On-Device Preferences: Settings, display configurations, user toggles, and saved states are saved strictly in Android Room SQLite or EncryptedSharedPreferences on your physical hardware.
  • Zero Cloud Infrastructure: MHPartners does not maintain remote user databases or backend servers for this application. There are no accounts to create, passwords to remember, or profile data harvested.
  • Zero Keystroke / Behavioral Logging: We do not track, intercept, or log keystrokes, typed text, screen usage frequency, geographic coordinates, or interaction histories.
  • Local File Export & Backup: Any backup, export, or theme files generated within the application are saved directly to your local file system where you explicitly specify.

3. Android System Permissions

To execute its native functions on Android (API 26+), Balochi Keyboard may request minimal operating system permissions. Each permission is requested transparently:

Microphone & Voice Typing (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used solely when you tap the microphone button on the keyboard to dictate text via voice speech-to-text. Spoken audio is converted into text ephemerally in real-time and is never recorded, saved to disk, or sent to studio servers.

Zero Keystroke Logging

All typed text, words, and passwords are processed strictly in volatile device memory to render characters. No keystrokes are ever logged, saved, or transmitted to any server.

Input Method Service (BIND_INPUT_METHOD)

Required by the Android operating system to operate as a system keyboard and input method editor (IME) across your installed applications.

Vibration & Haptics (VIBRATE)

Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback on key presses. Can be customized or disabled anytime in the application's keyboard settings.

100% Offline / Zero Remote Transmission

Balochi Keyboard does not transmit personal dictionaries, custom shortcuts, or user preferences to any external cloud infrastructure.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Activity

Because Balochi Keyboard is designed to operate locally on-device, it does not transmit data to advertising networks, user-profiling exchanges, or tracking services.

Standard operating system integrations provided by Google Play Services (such as APK license verification, in-app billing for studio support, or anonymous crash reports aggregated by Google Play Console) operate under Google's Privacy Policy. MHPartners has no direct access to individual Google Play account IDs.

5. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

Since all data is localized to your physical hardware, you maintain perpetual and irrevocable authority over your records:

  • In-App Controls: You may edit, clear, or reset application preferences directly inside the app's settings menu at any time.
  • System Clear Data: Navigating to Android Settings → Apps → Balochi Keyboard → Storage → Clear Storage / Data will completely wipe all local SQLite databases, caches, and key-value stores.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application removes all application-private storage sandbox partitions permanently from your device.

6. Children's Privacy & Family Safety

Balochi Keyboard is a general-audience utility suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. If you have reason to believe a minor has provided data, please contact us immediately for assistance.
