Phone Cleaner

Effective Date: 26 November 2026
Sections: 1. Overview 2. Data Collection 3. Permissions 4. Third Parties 5. Data Deletion 6. Contact

Zero Telemetry & Absolute Device Privacy

Phone Cleaner operates entirely on your physical hardware. MHPartners does not collect, sell, harvest, or transmit your personal data, keystrokes, app preferences, or telemetry to external servers. Your device is your sanctuary.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the official Android application Phone Cleaner (package: net.vertexapps.phonecleaner), engineered by Vertex Apps and published on Google Play by MHPartners (“Studio”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

Phone Cleaner is an on-device storage optimization utility that scans junk files, residual caches, duplicate media, and large files locally on your hardware. For privacy questions, contact privacy@vertexapps.net or support@mhpartners.com.

2. Information Collection and On-Device Scanning

Phone Cleaner itself collects zero personally identifiable information (PII) and never uploads your files.

  • 100% Local File & Storage Scanning: All junk detection, duplicate photo matching, large-file discovery, and cache cleaning occur exclusively on your physical device. Your photos, videos, documents, and personal file names never leave your phone.
  • No User Accounts or Cloud Backups: There is no account creation, login, email, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone, or location access.
  • Local App Preferences: Cleaning preferences, whitelist/ignore lists, and UI settings are saved strictly in local device storage and removed upon uninstall or clearing app data.

3. Android System Permissions

To scan and clean storage on Android (API 26+), Phone Cleaner may request the following permissions when you use specific cleaning tools:

Storage & Media Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_*)

Used strictly locally to scan and remove junk files, residual caches, duplicate media, empty folders, and large files you select for deletion.

App Storage & Usage Stats (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS / QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

Used locally to calculate app storage and cache sizes and help you uninstall unused apps. Your installed app list is never sent off your device.

Network Access (INTERNET / Google AdMob)

All storage scanning and cleaning tools work offline. Network access is used solely by Google AdMob to load banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads.

Zero Sensitive Sensor Access

Phone Cleaner never requests contacts, call logs, SMS, camera, microphone, or GPS location.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Phone Cleaner displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements powered by Google AdMob. We do not operate proprietary analytics or tracking servers; Google AdMob is the sole third-party advertising SDK integrated into the application.

Information Processed by Google AdMob

When you use Phone Cleaner with an active internet connection, the Google AdMob SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Online & Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and system device identifiers.
  • Device & Operating System Data: Device model, hardware manufacturer, operating system version, system language, and network connection type.
  • Ad Performance & Usage Telemetry: Ad impressions, viewability metrics, click interactions, frequency capping counters, and ad diagnostics.

Purposes & Google Policies: This data is used by Google AdMob for delivering ads, measuring performance, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and (where permitted by consent) personalized ads. Review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Opt-Out & Regional Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): Reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Where required by GDPR/UK GDPR, users are presented with a consent dialogue via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Consistent with Google Play Data Safety, Phone Cleaner declares Device or other IDs and App interactions as collected and shared with Google AdMob. We never sell personal data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All app preferences remain locally on your device and are permanently deleted when you clear app storage (Android Settings → Apps → Phone Cleaner → Storage) or uninstall the application. AdMob data retention is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

6. Children’s Privacy

Phone Cleaner is a general-audience device utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in compliance with COPPA.
