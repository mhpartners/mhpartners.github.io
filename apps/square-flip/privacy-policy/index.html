Privacy Policy

Square Flip

Effective Date: October 2026
Sections: 1. Overview 2. Data Collection 3. Permissions 4. Third Parties 5. Data Deletion 6. Contact

Zero Telemetry & Absolute Device Privacy

Square Flip operates entirely on your physical hardware. MHPartners does not collect, sell, harvest, or transmit your personal data, keystrokes, app preferences, or telemetry to external servers. Your device is your sanctuary.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the official Android mobile game Square Flip (package: net.vertexapps.squareflip), engineered by Vertex Apps and published on Google Play by MHPartners (“Studio”, “we”, “our”, or “us”).

Square Flip is an elegant neon grid puzzle game featuring 120 Classic levels, 20 Mosaic Art picture puzzles, and 60-second Blitz Rush runs. Our engineering philosophy is anchored in minimal data collection, local on-device execution, and total privacy transparency. For any privacy-related inquiries, players can reach us directly at privacy@vertexapps.net or support@mhpartners.com.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Square Flip itself collects zero personally identifiable information (PII). We do not track, harvest, or log any personal data:

  • No User Accounts or Sign-In: There is no account registration, login, profile creation, name, email address, or phone number required. You can jump in and play immediately.
  • No Sensitive Hardware Access: The game does not access your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or device storage files.
  • No In-App Purchases: There are no financial transactions, paywalls, or payment card details stored or processed.
  • Zero Proprietary Analytics: We do not operate proprietary tracking, behavioral analytics, or user profiling telemetry. The only third-party service integrated is Google AdMob for advertisements.
  • Stored Exclusively on Your Device: All gameplay progress (unlocked levels across 120 Classic puzzles and 20 Mosaic Art picture puzzles, stars earned, best move counts), Blitz Rush high scores, and settings (sound effects toggle, music toggle, music volume slider) are saved locally on your phone (Room SQLite / EncryptedSharedPreferences). None of this data is transmitted to Vertex Apps, MHPartners, or any external servers. Local data is completely deleted when the app is uninstalled or when you clear data in Android Settings.

3. Android System Permissions

To execute its native functions on Android (API 26+), Square Flip requests only minimal operating system permissions:

Tactile Flip Haptics (VIBRATE)

Used solely to deliver crisp haptic vibration feedback when flipping tiles, completing patterns, and solving puzzle stages. Can be toggled off anytime in the game settings.

Local Progress & High Scores

All 120 Classic levels, 20 Mosaic Art puzzles, Blitz Rush high scores, 3-star ratings, and audio volume sliders are saved strictly on your local hardware.

Zero Personal Data & No Account

Square Flip requires no user account, registration, login, email address, or identity verification. Jump in and play immediately with full privacy.

Network Access (INTERNET / Ad Delivery)

All puzzle modes are fully playable offline without an internet connection. Network connectivity is used solely by Google AdMob to load banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Square Flip displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements through Google AdMob. We do not operate proprietary analytics or tracking services; Google AdMob is the sole third-party advertising SDK integrated into the game.

Information Processed by Google AdMob

When you play Square Flip with an active internet connection, the Google AdMob SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Online & Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and system device identifiers.
  • Device & Operating System Data: Device model, hardware manufacturer, operating system version, system language, and network connection type.
  • Ad Performance & Usage Telemetry: Ad impressions, viewability metrics, click interactions, frequency capping counters, and ad diagnostics.

Purposes of Ad Data Processing: This data is utilized by Google AdMob for delivering advertisements, measuring ad campaign performance, enforcing frequency capping (avoiding repetitive ads), combating ad fraud and abuse, and—where permitted by user consent—serving personalized advertisements.

Google Privacy Policy & Partner Sites: For complete details regarding how Google collects and uses information in partner applications, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Player Privacy Controls & Personalized Ads Opt-Out: Players maintain total control over advertising personalization. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device system settings:

Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads)

Regional Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): In compliance with GDPR and UK data protection frameworks, players in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with a consent dialogue powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are displayed, and can adjust or withdraw consent choices anytime in the app's Settings menu.

Fair, Optional Rewarded Advertisements: Square Flip offers optional rewarded video ads. Players may voluntarily choose to watch a sponsored video to unlock extra hints (up to 2 per level) or earn +15 seconds when a Blitz Rush run expires. Rewarded ads are 100% voluntary and never mandatory.

Google Play Data Safety Alignment: In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, Square Flip declares Device or other IDs and App interactions as collected and shared with Google AdMob for advertising and fraud prevention purposes. We never sell personal data.

5. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

Since all game data is localized to your physical hardware, you maintain perpetual and irrevocable authority over your records:

  • In-App Controls: You may edit, clear, or reset game preferences and audio settings directly inside the in-game Settings menu at any time.
  • System Clear Data: Navigating to Android Settings → Apps → Square Flip → Storage → Clear Storage / Data will completely wipe all local SQLite databases, level progression, and high scores.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application removes all application-private storage sandbox partitions permanently from your device. AdMob advertising data retention is governed by Google's privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy & Family Safety

Square Flip is designed as a casual logic puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13. Vertex Apps and MHPartners do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and our declared target audience in the Google Play Console. If you have reason to believe that a child has provided data, please contact us at privacy@vertexapps.net so we can take immediate action.
