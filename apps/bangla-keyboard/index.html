PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Built for precision, speed, and focus

Bangla Keyboard with English is a smart Bengali keyboard for Android. Type easily and naturally in Bangla and English, access full Bengali vowels, consonants, and Yuktakkhor (যুক্তাক্ষর), get smart suggestions and autocomplete, use dual voice typing, translate languages, improve writing with AI tools, calculate without leaving chat, and customize your keyboard your way.

⌨️ Smart Bangla & English Keyboard: Type in Bangla and English from one unified keyboard and quickly toggle between languages while chatting, writing emails, searching, taking notes, or using social media.

🔤 Full Bangla Alphabet & Phonetic Typing: Type easily with phonetic English-to-Bangla or traditional layout. Quick access to swaroborno, banjonborno, yukta letters, and Bengali numeral options.

✨ AI Writing Assistant: Enhance writing directly from the keyboard: rephrase text, fix grammar, adjust tone, and generate prompt-based responses.

💡 Smart Suggestions & Word Prediction: Type faster with smart suggestions and predictive word completion tailored for conversational and formal Bangla.

🎙️ Bangla & English Voice Typing: Speak naturally instead of typing in both Bangla and English with accurate on-device transcription.

🌐 Built-in Translator: Translate between Bangla, English, and other major languages directly from the keyboard.

🎨 100+ Designer Keyboard Themes: Choose from rich gradient, AMOLED dark, minimal, artistic, and cultural themes with customizable key styles.

At MHPartners, every release adheres to our strict atelier engineering manifesto: zero third-party tracking libraries, zero invasive advertising banners, and instant sub-millisecond cold starts. We leverage Kotlin Coroutines and native Jetpack Compose to deliver responsive tactile touch feedback.
