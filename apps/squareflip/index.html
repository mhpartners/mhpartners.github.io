PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Built for geometric logic, flow state, and speed

Square Flip is an elegant and addictive neon grid puzzle game engineered for Android (package: net.vertexapps.squareflip). Draw a square to flip every tile inside it, turn the whole board white, recreate artistic mosaic shapes, and race against the clock in high-intensity Blitz runs!

🧩 Three Ways to Play:
• Classic: 120 hand-made puzzles. Draw a square to flip every tile inside it, and turn the whole board white. Grids grow from 4×4 up to 9×9.
• Mosaic Art: 20 picture puzzles. Flip tiles to recreate a target shape: hearts, stars, crowns, keys, a spiral and more.
• Blitz Rush: A 60-second race against the clock. Solve as many puzzles as you can and chain quick solves into combos (up to 5×). Boards get bigger as you go, and you can chase your high score.

💡 Smart Help & Guided Learning:
• Hints that read your board: they show the best next move from where you are, not from the start. If starting over is faster, the game tells you.
• Unlimited undo and instant restart: experiment without fear.
• A guided first level and a How to Play guide to jump straight into the action.

⭐ Progress and Challenge:
• 3-star ratings: beat the target move count for a perfect score.
• Statistics: best moves, stars earned and Blitz records.
• Saved automatically, and fully playable offline. Ads need a connection; the game doesn't.

✨ Look and Feel:
• A glowing neon look: glossy tiles, animated backgrounds, flip bursts and confetti celebrations.
• Relaxing background music and satisfying sound effects, each with its own setting, plus a music volume slider.
• Haptic feedback on every move.

🎁 Fair, Optional Rewards:
• Watch an ad, only if you want to: to unlock extra hints (up to 2 per level) or get +15 seconds when a Blitz run ends.
