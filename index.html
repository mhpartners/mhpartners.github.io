Our Atelier Privacy & Data Ethics Manifesto

At MHPartners, we adhere to the strict principle of zero unnecessary surveillance. We believe modern mobile utilities must respect user sanctuary. None of our keyboard applications record or transmit keystrokes, our acoustic utilities discard sound data ephemerally in RAM, and our personalization apps never read biometric enclave data. All application records remain under your perpetual hardware control.
